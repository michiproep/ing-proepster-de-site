Product · Identity Provider

Moving off Azure AD B2C – and Entra External ID doesn't fit?

Azure AD B2C is no longer sold to new customers, and its successor does not cover everything that custom policies made possible. TheIdP is an enterprise identity provider that takes over where B2C leaves off – familiar for your apps, extended where B2C was always too tight, and run wherever you want.

In development · pilot customers welcome

Migration without rewriting your apps

Login flows selected by authority URL like B2C policies, B2C-compatible claims, app registrations in the style of Entra ID. For most applications the switch is a new authority – not a new project.

B2C-compatible claims · Entra-style app registrations · OpenID Connect

Token exchange between APIs

An API exchanges the incoming user token for a token for the next API – only between permitted pairs, with the full call chain recorded in the token. What B2C never offered is built in.

RFC 8693 · delegation chains · single-audience tokens

B2B and B2C in one system

Business customers sign in with their own Entra ID or any OIDC provider, found automatically by email domain and linked safely. Consumers use local accounts with passkeys, authenticator app or email code.

Home realm discovery · federation per customer · passkeys · MFA

Adaptable – in code, not in XML

Login flows are real code instead of custom-policy XML – versioned, tested and released like any software. Branding and campaigns, translations, email templates and claim mappings are maintained in the console; hooks allow fraud checks or step-up rules.

Flows as code · campaigns · extension hooks

Failover and self-hosting

Run it yourself – in your data centre or any cloud. A second site, even with another cloud provider, stays in sync; switching over is a DNS change and one click, and users keep working.

Two sites · Azure, AWS, Google Cloud · on-premises · no lock-in

Support that sees what the customer sees

Authorised staff can sign in to an application on behalf of a customer user – only where permitted, time-limited, clearly marked in the token and fully audited.

Impersonation · time-limited · audit trail
Ask your identity provider

“Why couldn't jane.doe@customer.example sign in this morning?”

She was sent to her company's sign-in (found by email domain). The company's identity provider returned an error at 08:14 – reason code FEDERATION_ERROR. Its health check shows an expired certificate since yesterday. Next step: ask the customer's IT to renew it; a test link for them is ready.

Built-in MCP server: support asks in plain language from an AI assistant such as Claude – read-only, limited to the person's role, every call audited.

AI & Automation

Software that grows with you

Conventional software stands still once the project is finished. With AI, you can build a cycle in which your application keeps getting better – driven by the people who work with it every day.

  1. 1

    Make a request

    Report a bug or an idea directly in the application – with a click, a form or in conversation with the built-in AI assistant.

  2. 2

    AI evaluates

    The AI assesses feasibility, effort and risks, and asks targeted follow-up questions if anything is unclear.

  3. 3

    You decide

    Nothing happens without your approval: you accept the proposal, refine it or reject it.

  4. 4

    AI implements

    The change is programmed and safeguarded by automated tests – only what passes every check moves on.

  5. 5

    New release

    The improved version is deployed automatically. You try it out – and the cycle starts again.

↻  and round again

  • Short path from idea to solutionSmall improvements are often available within hours instead of weeks.
